How it works

Three parts. One habit.

A contact switch in the lid knows the moment the box is opened or closed. Everything else runs in the cloud, so the people who care never need to babysit it.

1

Open the box, take your dose

Opening the lid during the daily window is the check-in. Nothing to press, unlock, or charge for the user. The box logs it and goes quiet.

2

The box watches the clock

You set the window once, for example 8:00 to 10:00 AM. If the lid has not been opened by the end of the window, DailyDoseBox knows the box stayed shut.

3

It speaks up, then escalates

A gentle reminder first. Still shut? It texts your people, plays a spoken message, and can place a phone call. You choose the ladder.

If the box stays shut

You decide what happens, and in what order.

Every step is optional and every contact is yours to set. Calm by default, loud only when it matters.

DailyDoseBox reports whether the box was opened, not whether a dose was taken. It is a daily check-in aid, not a medical device.
