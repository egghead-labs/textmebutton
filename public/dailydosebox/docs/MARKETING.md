# DailyDoseBox — Marketing Kit (Maya, 2026-06-05)

## Positioning
DailyDoseBox is a handsome wooden pill box for adult children and caregivers worried about an aging parent missing daily meds. Unlike clinical dispensers (Hero ~$45/mo, MedMinder ~$40-65/mo) that look medical, charge monthly, and need an app, it sits on the nightstand like furniture and works with zero daily effort: opening the lid is the check-in. If the box stays shut past the window it escalates on its own (chime → text → spoken message → caregiver text → call). Setup is a memorable 3-word code; one-time purchase, not a subscription.

**April Dunford:**
- Alternatives: doing nothing, daily "did you take it?" calls, clinical dispensers, door sensors.
- Unique: looks like furniture, no app, 3-word setup, self-escalating ladder, one-time price.
- Value+proof: peace of mind without nagging (the box texts YOU) and without a forever subscription (vs $480-780/yr clinical).
- Best-fit: adult children of independent-but-forgetful parents; small caregivers wanting dignity not a hospital look.
- Category: a daily medication check-in aid for the home (not a medical device, not a dispenser).

## Pricing copy (PROPOSED)
Founding banner: "Founding pre-order: first 500 boxes ship at launch pricing with free lifetime alerts. No subscription, ever."
- **The Box** $99 — one person. Lid check-in, 3-word setup, full escalation ladder, free lifetime alerts.
- **The Care Pair** $169 — two boxes, up to 5 contacts, custom voice message, priority support.
- **The Caregiver Set** $399 — 5 boxes each with its own 3-word code, manage from one login, priority onboarding.
Anchor: clinical rivals run $480-780/yr; The Box pays for itself in 2-3 months, then costs nothing.
Risk flag: "free lifetime alerts" carries cellular/SMS cost forever — if not viable, use an optional $4/mo connectivity add-on instead.
